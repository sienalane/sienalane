Word Ladder

Change one letter at a time to get from the green word to the gold word.

How to play

  • Type a real word that changes exactly one letter from the word above it.
  • Keep the letters in the same order. No adding, removing or swapping letters.
  • Reach the gold word to win. Try to match par, the fewest steps possible.
  • Stuck? A hint fills in the next word on a shortest path.
