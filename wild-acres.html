Wild Acres

Start with a petting barn and grow it into a world-class zoo.

0Coins

How to play

  • Tap Lead a tour to earn your first coins.
  • Adopt animals to bring in visitors. Each animal earns coins every second, even while you're away.
  • Every 10, 25, 50, 100 and 200 animals of a kind doubles what they earn.
  • Open new habitats to unlock bigger animals. Buy upgrades to multiply your earnings.
  • When a 🍼 pops up, tap it fast. A baby animal was born and visitors are lining up.
  • Later on, donate your zoo to the Conservation Fund to start over with permanent bonus stars.
  • Your zoo saves automatically in this browser.
