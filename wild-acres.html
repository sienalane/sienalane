Wild Acres

Start with a petting barn and grow it into a world-class zoo.

0Coins

How to play

  • Tap Lead a tour to earn your first coins. Adopt animals to bring in visitors. Animals earn coins every second, even while you're away.
  • Every 10, 25, 50, 100 and 200 animals of a kind doubles what they earn. Open new habitats for bigger animals, and buy upgrades to multiply your earnings.
  • Your first animal of each kind gets a name. Tap the name to change it.
  • Come back every day for a gift. It grows the more days in a row you visit.
  • Visitor requests pop up now and then. Meet them in 10 minutes for a reward. Rare animals also appear for a few minutes. Adopt them for a permanent bonus.
  • Keepers adopt animals for you and keep their habitat earning at full speed while you're away.
  • Every new species unlocks a fact card. Every achievement adds 2% to your earnings for good.
  • The zoo follows your real clock. At night it gets dark and the music slows down.
  • When a 🍼 pops up, tap it fast. A baby was born and visitors are lining up.
  • Later on, donate your zoo to the Conservation Fund to start over with permanent bonus stars. Rare animals, cards and achievements stay with you.
  • Turn on Sound to hear your zoo. Every kind of animal adds its own part to the song. Your zoo saves automatically in this browser.
