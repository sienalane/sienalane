Four by Four

Find four groups of four words that belong together.

How to play

  • Pick four words that share something in common, then press Submit.
  • Groups go from yellow (most straightforward) to purple (trickiest).
  • Watch out for words that seem to fit more than one group.
  • You can make four mistakes before the game ends.
