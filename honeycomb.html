Honeycomb

Make words from the seven letters. Every word needs the gold center letter.

You have found 0 words

    How to play

    • Words must be at least 4 letters and include the center letter.
    • Letters can be used more than once.
    • 4-letter words are worth 1 point. Longer words get 1 point per letter.
    • A word that uses all seven letters is a pangram and earns 7 bonus points.
    • Type on your keyboard or tap the letters. Climb the ranks up to Hive Master.
