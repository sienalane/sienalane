Guess Five

Find the hidden five-letter word in six tries.

How to play

  • Type any five-letter word and press Enter.
  • Green means the letter is in the right spot.
  • Gold means the letter is in the word but in a different spot.
  • Gray means the letter isn't in the word.
  • Daily is the same word for everyone each day. Unlimited gives you a fresh word whenever you want.
