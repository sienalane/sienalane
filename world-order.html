How to play

  • Each turn is one year. You get 3 actions per year, then press End Year.
  • At home: grow the economy, build the military, fund research, or invest in your people to raise stability.
  • Abroad: tap any country to see it, then improve relations, sign trade deals, form alliances, send sanctions or declare war.
  • You can attack neighbors and nearby countries. Big militaries can reach anywhere.
  • Wars play out each year. Win enough battles and you take territory. Allies join their friends' wars.
  • Keep stability up. If it hits zero, your government falls and the game ends.
  • The game runs until 2076. Your progress saves automatically in this browser.
  • Country stats are rough approximations for fun, not real data, and the game doesn't simulate real politics.
